Poops is a straightforward, no-bullshit bundler and static site generator for the web. It
compiles JavaScript/TypeScript (esbuild), Sass/CSS (Dart Sass + PostCSS), and templates
(Nunjucks or Liquid) with Markdown and front matter — plus responsive images, collections,
search index, sitemap and navigation trees, all from a single `poops.json`.

The pages below are grouped by area: quick-start guides, full static-site walkthroughs, the
configuration reference, and the changelog.
